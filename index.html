Manifesto

You own the web. The attacker is the fly.

AI agents read data, call tools, move money and change infrastructure — often with all-or-nothing permissions and no record anyone trusts afterward. ARACHNODE is the control plane that sits between every agent and every action it tries to take: it verifies who is acting, decides whether the action is allowed, and keeps a tamper-evident record of what happened.

The metaphor is literal. You are the spider; your web is the control plane. Each strand is a governed connection. A vibration on the strand is telemetry. When a rogue agent touches the web, the whole structure feels it — it is lured down a false strand, and the trapdoor closes over it.

Most security products are born inside security companies. ARACHNODE was born somewhere else: in the mind of a retired nutraceutical food scientist who spent his first career learning how to keep dangerous things out of the food supply.

From 1996 to 1997 he worked at Rutgers University’s Center for Advanced Food Technology (CAFT). Food science teaches a particular kind of vigilance: you assume something harmful will eventually get in. You don’t only build walls; you build detection, containment and traceability. When a contaminant appears, you isolate the batch, study it, record every step, and protect everything downstream.

In the late summer of 2026 he turned that same thinking on the newest danger of all: AI agents that no longer just answer questions, but take real actions inside real systems.

What if, instead of just shutting the door on a suspicious agent, a system could quietly capture it, learn what it was after, and then trap it, while the real system stays untouched?

That question became ARACHNODE. A spider doesn’t chase its prey; it builds a structure that senses disturbance, holds what enters, and waits. The Web is the Gateway. The Eight Eyes are the Mirror Maze. The Trapdoor is quarantine. And the Master Discernment Key keeps a human in charge.

Age did not slow this work down. Experience shaped it.

— Bruce Derrick “Kuumba” Williams, Founder
